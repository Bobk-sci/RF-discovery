# A 3.2-3.6 GHz GaN Doherty Power Amplifier Module Based on a Compact Low-Loss Combiner.

*journal non renseigné — 2025*

## Résumé (texte d'origine)

In this paper, a 3.2-3.6 GHz two-stage Doherty power amplifier (PA) module is proposed for fifth-generation (5G) massive multiple-input multiple-output (MIMO) base stations. A detailed design method and procedure for a compact and low-loss combiner suitable for the Doherty PA module are introduced. Based on the proposed combiner, a Doherty PA module is implemented using gallium nitride (GaN) transistors and surface-mounted devices (SMDs) with a packaged size of 8 × 8 mm<sup>2</sup>. The proposed two-stage Doherty PA module achieves a 3 dB small-signal bandwidth of 3.1-3.9 GHz and a peak gain of 31.7 dB. From 3.2 to 3.6 GHz, the saturated output power is 40.4-41.1 dBm. Moreover, the measured saturated drain efficiency (DE) and 8 dB power back-off (PBO) DE reach 51-56.6% and 45.5-48.6%, respectively.
