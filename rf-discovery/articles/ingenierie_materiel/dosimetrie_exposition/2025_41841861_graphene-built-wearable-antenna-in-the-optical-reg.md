# Graphene-built wearable antenna in the optical regime: frequency-tunable dual-band twin-port design with a metasurface reflector and low SAR.

*Applied optics — 2025*

## Résumé (texte d'origine)

A small, wearable, two-port, dual-band antenna built on a polyimide substrate is shown in this study. Twin C-formed, graphene-made structures create dual frequency bands. Above 30 dB, terminal isolation is provided via polarization variety. For 1 g and 10 g tissue models, a metasurface reflector layer under the radiator improves gain to 3.85 and 4.91 dBi while reducing specific absorption rate (SAR) by more than 90%. The radiator frequency may be adjusted due to graphene's change in chemical potential. With simulated data (HFSS/CST) verifying functioning in 2.25-2.75 THz and 3.45-4.25 THz, the antenna maintains steady performance during bending, making it appropriate for THz wireless applications.
