# Dual-Frequency and Pattern Reconfigurable Wireless Communication System for Adaptive Cross-Body Biotelemetry

*journal non renseigné — 2026*

## Résumé (texte d'origine)

<title>Abstract</title>  <p>This paper presents a micrometer-thick, conformal, and mechanically flexible reconfigurable electromagnetic wireless communication circuit fabricated using a laser-combined wet-etching additive manufacturing process. The proposed flexible on-board antenna integrates frequency and radiation-pattern reconfigurability to enable dynamic electromagnetic control. Real-time mode switching is achieved using PIN diodes controlled by an onboard microcontroller unit, allowing the device to adapt its operating frequency and radiation characteristics in response to changing system requirements. Experimental results demonstrate frequency reconfigurability from 2.388 to 2.467 GHz within the 2.45 GHz ISM band, together with directional-to-omnidirectional radiation-pattern switching and beam steering. The device maintains stable electromagnetic performance under bending in multiple planes, with mechanical compliance demonstrated down to a bending radius of 40 mm.  To evaluate implant-to-wearable communication performance, full-wave transmission coefficient (S21) simulations under translational and orientational misalignments between the implantable transmitter and external receiver demonstrate transmission-loss mitigation of up to 5.41 dB, with good agreement observed in in vitro measurements using porcine tissue. An electromagnetic region-of-interest-based evaluation method is further introduced to quantify mode-dependent performance within the coupling region. These results highlight the potential of laser-combined wet-etching additive fabrication for flexible reconfigurable antennas and mechanically adaptive electromagnetic systems for wearable, biomedical, sensing, communication, and other body-centric applications.</p>
