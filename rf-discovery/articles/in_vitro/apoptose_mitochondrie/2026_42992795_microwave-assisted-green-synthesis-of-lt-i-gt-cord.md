# Microwave-assisted green synthesis of &lt;i&gt;Cordyceps militaris&lt;/i&gt;-derived silver nanoparticles and conjugation with salinomycin for the enhanced anticancer and antimicrobial activity.

*journal non renseigné — 2026*

## Résumé (texte d'origine)

Green synthesis of silver nanoparticles (AgNPs) using extract of a medicinal mushroom <i>Cordyceps militaris (C. militaris)</i> has emerged as a promising alternative to conventional nanoparticle synthesis methods. In this study, microwave-assisted fungal-mediated biosynthesized AgNPs were conjugated with salinomycin (Sal), an anticancer compound with activity against cancer stem cell populations. The extract of <i>C. militaris</i> effectively mediated the reduction of silver ions, leading to the formation of AgNPs that exhibited a characteristic peak at 417 nm in the UV-visible spectrum. DLS, TEM, SEM-EDS and FTIR analysis verified the morphology, stability, and functional groups. The antibacterial activity of the AgNP, Sal, and Sal-AgNP was evaluated against <i>E. coli</i> and <i>S. aureus.</i> Additionally, the cytotoxicity was determined by MTT assay in L-929 and MCF-7 cell lines, revealing that the Sal-AgNP conjugate, substantially decreased cell viability compared with individual treatments, with a more noticeable cytotoxic effect observed in MCF-7 cell lines, indicating enhanced anticancer activity. The findings of Annexin V-PI double staining imply that conjugated AgNPs enhance irreversible cell death and salinomycin-induced apoptosis. Overall, salinomycin-conjugated AgNPs enhanced the observed antimicrobial and anticancer activities under the tested in vitro conditions, highlighting their potential as a promising nanotherapeutic platform that warrants further investigation in preclinical studies.
