# Preoperative risk stratification for anastomotic leakage after gastrectomy: a model based on dual‑energy CT extracellular volume fraction and sarcopenia.

*journal non renseigné — 2026*

## Résumé (texte d'origine)

<h4>Purpose</h4>To develop a purely preoperative risk stratification model for anastomotic leakage (AL) after radical gastrectomy using clinical, dual-energy CT (DECT), and body composition variables available before surgery.<h4>Methods</h4>This retrospective study included 439 patients with gastric cancer who underwent radical gastrectomy. Preoperative clinical, DECT, and body composition data were collected. A domain-based predictor selection strategy was used, followed by Firth penalized logistic regression to account for the low event rate. Internal validation was performed using 1000 bootstrap resamples, with an additional full-pipeline bootstrap sensitivity analysis. Model performance was assessed by discrimination, calibration, decision curve analysis (DCA), and risk stratification.<h4>Results</h4>AL occurred in 27 patients (6.15%). The final model included cardia tumor location (OR = 4.44), higher ECV (OR = 1.09 per 1%), and sarcopenia (OR = 3.66). The model showed good discrimination, with an apparent AUC of 0.853 and a bootstrap-corrected AUC of 0.843. Full-pipeline bootstrap sensitivity analysis yielded an optimism-corrected AUC of 0.833. Calibration was acceptable, with a Brier score of 0.050, and DCA suggested potential net benefit. Observed AL rates increased stepwise across low-risk (< 3%), intermediate-risk (3%-10%), and high-risk (≥ 10%) groups: 1.23%, 5.13%, and 22.78%, respectively. The high-risk group comprised 18.0% of the cohort but accounted for 66.7% of all AL events.<h4>Conclusion</h4>A concise model integrating tumor location, DECT-derived ECV, and sarcopenia showed favorable performance for preoperative AL risk stratification after gastrectomy. This internally validated model may help preoperative AL risk stratification, but multicenter external validation and impact analysis are required before implementation.
