# Compact Cross Dipole Metamaterial-Inspired THz Antenna for Non-invasive early Breast Tumor Detection

*journal non renseigné — 2026*

## Résumé (texte d'origine)

<title>Abstract</title>  <p>The design of a high-performance terahertz microstrip patch antenna that incorporates a cross-dipole reflector into the ground plane and serves as a metamaterial-inspired Frequency Selective Surface is the main innovation of this work. This passive structural breakthrough uses a straightforward polyimide-gold architecture to produce improved radiation efficiency (94%) and gain (6.6 dBi) at 2.6 THz, in contrast to earlier adjustable methods that relied on graphene biasing. Additionally, by simultaneously analyzing the reflection coefficient (S₁₁), transmission coefficient (S₂₁), and Specific Absorption Rate (SAR) contrast, the antenna exhibits sensitive detection of incredibly small tumors (10 µm,15µm and 20µm radius), a multi-parameter diagnostic capability not previously reported in THz breast cancer detection studies..</p>
