# Application-dependent transmit power for auto-induced uplink exposure assessment from in-situ mobile phone usage in four European countries.

*Environmental research — 2026*

## Résumé (texte d'origine)

Radiofrequency electromagnetic field (RF-EMF) exposure from smartphones depends strongly on user behavior and network conditions, particularly in modern 4G and 5G networks due to increased spatio-temporal variability in uplink transmissions. Understanding exposure differences between realistic usage scenarios is essential for accurate personal exposure assessment. Previous studies mainly focused on legacy technologies or were limited to one country. This study quantifies and compares in-situ average transmit (Tx) powers for voice calls, WhatsApp voice calls, WhatsApp video calls, and uplink file transfers across four European countries, and analyzes how differences between applications, signal strength across locations, and exposure duration relate to uplink Tx power variability. Measurements were performed in 148 outdoor microenvironments in Belgium, France, Hungary, and Switzerland. Trained researchers executed four standardized usage scenarios while network monitoring tools (QualiPoc, Nemo) recorded network parameters from the phone. Uplink file transfers showed the highest Tx powers (median 19.50 dBm), followed by WhatsApp video calls (8.67 dBm) and WhatsApp voice calls (4.20 dBm). Voice calls resulted in the lowest Tx powers (0.27 dBm). 4G dominated usage in Belgium and Hungary, while Switzerland and France showed predominantly simultaneous 4G and 5G operation. The average Tx power exhibited increasing trends with duty cycle and throughput, and an inverse relationship with Reference Signal Received Power (RSRP). Uplink file transfer duration also depended strongly on RSRP. Uplink exposure was shown to be highly application- and location-dependent. These findings support improved personal RF-EMF exposure and dose assessment and provide the first multi-country comparison between applications including non-standalone 5G.
