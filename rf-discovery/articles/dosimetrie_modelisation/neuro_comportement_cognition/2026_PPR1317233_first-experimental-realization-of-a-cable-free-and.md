# First Experimental Realization of a Cable-Free and Battery-Free Spatial Power Grid Infrastructure

*journal non renseigné — 2026*

## Résumé (texte d'origine)

<title>Abstract</title>  <p>  Modern electronic platforms remain fundamentally constrained by wired AC/DC distribution infrastructure and electrochemical battery storage, which introduce mass overhead, thermal runaway risks, and ecological waste. Traditional wireless power transfer (WPT) architectures—such as near-field Qi magnetic induction (≤ 1 cm) and far-field radiative RF/microwave beams—fail to achieve room-scale delivery due to rapid free-space path loss (1/r2) and stringent Specific Absorption Rate (SAR) human safety limits. Here, we present the world’s first experimental realization of a cable-free and battery-free spatial power grid infrastructure based on Algorithmic Topological Resonance (ATR) and nonradiative evanescent wave coupling. Controlled by a spatiotemporal resonance condition RJM(pk,K) < 10  <sup>−12</sup>  , multi-element transmitter arrays embedded in surfaces construct a bounded evanescent field. Matched receiver devices equipped with a monolithic Finite-Field Power Management Integrated Circuit (PMIC) harvest power directly with a constant empirical transfer efficiency of 92.5±0.8% across indoor radii of 15m and dynamic mobility corridors up to 500 m. By integrating Adiabatic Charge Recovery Logic (ACRL), the receiver achieves 94.7% switching energy reclamation, maintaining thermal elevation below 0.05◦C and SAR exposure below 1.0 × 10  <sup>−4</sup>  W/kg (< 1/1000 of regulatory limits). Residual memory states are purged within 23.94 ms (<0.01% remanence) via Fowler- Nordheim field emission under a −5V bias. Building upon the theoretical framework of our companion study under peer review at Scientific Reports, this spatial grid establishes a scalable, intrinsically human-safe, zero-carbon power distribution paradigm that completely eliminates chemical batteries (0 kg) and physical charging cables.  </p>
