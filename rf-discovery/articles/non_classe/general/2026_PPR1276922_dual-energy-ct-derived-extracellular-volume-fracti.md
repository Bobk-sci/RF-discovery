# Dual-Energy CT-derived Extracellular Volume Fraction for Assessing Major Histologic Response to Neoadjuvant Chemotherapy in Breast Cancer

*journal non renseigné — 2026*

## Résumé (texte d'origine)

<title>Abstract</title>  <p>  <bold>Objective</bold>  : To evaluate dual-energy CT (DECT)-derived extracellular volume (ECV) fraction for assessing major histologic response (MHR) to neoadjuvant chemotherapy (NAC) in breast cancer.  <bold>Materials and Methods</bold>  : This retrospective study included 48 women (mean age, 50.0 years ± 10.2) who underwent DECT before and after NAC between 2021 and 2025. Patients were categorized into MHR (Miller-Payne grades 4-5; n = 21) and non-MHR (grades 1-3; n = 27) groups. ECV fraction was calculated using hematocrit-corrected CT attenuation values.  <bold>Results</bold>  : Post-treatment ECV fraction was lower in the MHR group than in the non-MHR group in both the arterial phase (median, 0.234 vs 0.448; P = .002) and venous phase (median, 0.391 vs 0.783; P < .001). The median reduction in venous ECV fraction was 1.294 in the MHR group versus 0.367 in the non-MHR group (Cohen d = 1.80,95% CI: 1.13–2.47).  <bold>Conclusion</bold>  : DECT-derived ECV fraction and its reduction after NAC were associated with MHR in patients with breast cancer after NAC.  </p>
