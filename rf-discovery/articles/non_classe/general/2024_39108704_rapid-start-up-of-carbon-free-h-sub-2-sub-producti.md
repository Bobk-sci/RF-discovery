# Rapid start-up of carbon-free H<sub>2</sub> production by ammonia oxidative decomposition over Co/Ce<sub>0.5</sub>Zr<sub>0.5</sub>O<sub>2</sub> with microwave irradiation.

*journal non renseigné — 2024*

## Résumé (texte d'origine)

Hydrogen is a promising combustion improver for use with ammonia fuels, but a cost-effective method for easily producing hydrogen from ammonia at a high rate has yet to be developed. Here, we show that microwave irradiation instantly triggers oxidative decomposition of ammonia over a Co/Ce<sub>0.5</sub>Zr<sub>0.5</sub>O<sub>2</sub> catalyst to produce hydrogen at a high rate. The microwave irradiation rapidly heats the inside of the catalyst from room temperature to the catalytic auto-ignition temperature of ammonia, thus initiating exothermic oxidative decomposition of ammonia to produce hydrogen. This method provides a highly efficient means of producing hydrogen for potential use in a carbon-free, ammonia-fueled power generation process.
