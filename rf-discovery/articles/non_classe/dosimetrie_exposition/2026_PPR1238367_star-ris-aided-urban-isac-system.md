# STAR-RIS Aided Urban ISAC System

*journal non renseigné — 2026*

## Résumé (texte d'origine)

Abstract—This correspondence presents an examination of an Integrated Sensing and Communication (ISAC) System in an urban setting utilizing a simultaneously transmitting and reflecting reconfigurable intelligent surfaces (STAR-RIS). We explore a scenario where a multi-antenna base station serves several single-antenna users and suggests equipping the STAR-RIS with cost-effective sensors to conduct sensing tasks instead of radar, thereby minimizing radar sensing path loss. To maximize user sum rate while fulfilling the minimum sensing function requirements, the power budget constraint, and the STAR-RIS reflecting coefficient constraints. We optimize the base station active beamforming and the passive beamforming of the STAR-RIS through a joint optimization process. An efficient algorithm based on alternating optimization and semidefinite relaxation (SDR) is proposed, simplifying a complex nonconvex optimization problem into two manageable convex problems for iterative resolution. Simulation outcomes demonstrate that the proposed STAR-RIS assisted ISAC system outperforms conventional RIS in urban scenarios.
