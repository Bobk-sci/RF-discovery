# Ground penetrating radar investigation for Land Subsidence along Dead Sea Highway 65, Jordan

*journal non renseigné — 2026*

## Résumé (texte d'origine)

<title>Abstract</title>  <p>This research examines the ground conditions around Wadi-Mujib and Jordan-Valley Highway, where infrastructure subsidence is imminent. By using ground-penetrating radar (GPR), the thicknesses and properties of subsurface layers were identified. To investigate underground voids, tunnels, and subsidence, a digital SIR-Voyager 20 radar system was mounted with 900 MHz and 400 MHz monostatic shielded antennas. Parallel GPR profiles were then collected. The survey identified a large depression to the east of the highway, which could lead to possible damage to infrastructure, and two shallow cavities located south of the subsidence area. Previous researchers have associated these cavities with issues of poor surface drainage. The results are essential for understanding the causes of subsidence and identifying remediation measures to prevent similar events in the future, and demonstrate the importance of using GPR for the detection and description of subsidence and related subsurface features for strengthening the resilience of critical infrastructure.</p>
